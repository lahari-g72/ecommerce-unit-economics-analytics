# Executive Business Memorandum

TO: VP of Growth & Commercial Operations  
FROM: Lahari G., E-Commerce Data Analyst  
DATE: August 27, 2026  
SUBJECT: Unit Economics Optimization, Repurchase Deceleration, and Channel CAC Allocation  

### 1. Context & Scope
An evaluation of 50,000+ orders across four primary product categories was conducted to audit unit economics, customer lifetime value (LTV), and marketing acquisition efficiency. The objective is to identify margin drag and provide data-backed interventions to improve Contribution Margin 2 (CM2).

### 2. Analytical Findings
1. Channel Quality Discrepancies: While Meta Ads and Influencer channels generate consistent gross volume, Paid Search and Organic search deliver significantly higher net unit margins, generating over INR 6.7M and INR 6.0M in CM2 profit respectively.
2. Customer Lifecycle Fatigue: Repurchase intervals expand consistently across repeat orders (14.9 days from 1st to 2nd order, increasing to 23.9 days by the 5th order). The 15-day mark represents the primary churn risk threshold.
3. Heavy Promotional Margin Drag: High promo discounting in Electronics and Beauty categories drives volume but reduces gross margin conversion.

### 3. Strategic Recommendations
1. Rebalance Marketing Budgets: Shift 20-25% of paid social budget to high-intent Paid Search to capture immediate demand at higher net margin.
2. Automated Retention Triggers: Establish automated lifecycle triggers on Day 12 post-purchase to arrest the deceleration cliff before Day 15.
3. Promo Discount Ceilings: Institute a hard cap of 12% on promotional discounts for high-velocity SKUs to protect unit contribution margins.
